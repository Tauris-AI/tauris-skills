# Petroleum Economics Review Checklist

Use this checklist after normalizing the source material into the system-agnostic economic model.

## Scope

- Asset, well, lease, or property identity.
- Effective date and case/scenario.
- Source system or source file, if known.
- Review question: conversion, reserves, economics, partner view, sale package, or sensitivity.

## Identity And Grouping

- Stable property/case ID.
- Display name and reporting labels.
- Reserve class/category.
- Operator, field, area, and other reporting attributes.
- Group/project membership.
- Incremental or rollup behavior.

## Volumes And Forecasts

- Forecast volumes and decline assumptions.
- Historical production handoff.
- Product coverage.
- Segment order and start dates.
- Forecast method, decline, limits, and terminal assumptions.
- Cumulative volume and EUR reasonableness.

## Pricing And Revenue

- Price deck, differentials, and escalation.
- Product realizations.
- Gas shrink, NGL yield, BTU, and plant assumptions.
- Transportation, processing, gathering, compression, or marketing deductions.

## Ownership

- Working interest.
- Net revenue interest.
- Royalties and burdens.
- Ownership dates.
- Payout, reversion, or qualified ownership behavior.
- Ownership variation by scenario, group, or project.

## Costs, Capital, And Taxes

- Operating costs, capital, abandonment, and taxes.
- Fixed vs variable LOE.
- Product-linked costs.
- Capital event timing and sign convention.
- Abandonment and future development costs.
- Severance, ad valorem, income, and other fiscal assumptions.

## Outputs

- Cash flow reasonableness.
- Reserve or volume consistency.
- NPV, payout, rate of return, and sensitivity reasonableness.
- Comparison against prior case or source system.
- Gross-to-net reconciliation.
- Revenue-to-cash-flow bridge.
- Expense, tax, and capital rollups.

## Review Output

List findings by severity and include the evidence or assumption behind each finding.

Use:

- Critical: likely changes economic conclusions or prevents reliance.
- High: material risk needing SME review.
- Medium: unresolved assumption or incomplete mapping.
- Low: cleanup, documentation, or presentation issue.
