# Petroleum Economics Review

Use this skill for structured review of petroleum engineering economic inputs and outputs.

## Workflow

1. Identify the asset, case, effective date, and economic question.
2. Separate inputs, calculations, and outputs.
3. Check units, dates, price assumptions, differentials, ownership, operating costs, taxes, and forecast assumptions.
4. Flag missing source context and unverified assumptions.
5. Produce a concise review with findings, evidence, and recommended next checks.
