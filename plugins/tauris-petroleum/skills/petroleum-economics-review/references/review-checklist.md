# Petroleum Economics Review Checklist

## Inputs

- Asset, well, lease, or property identity.
- Effective date and case/scenario.
- Forecast volumes and decline assumptions.
- Price deck, differentials, and escalation.
- Operating costs, capital, abandonment, and taxes.
- Working interest, net revenue interest, burdens, and ownership dates.

## Outputs

- Cash flow reasonableness.
- Reserve or volume consistency.
- NPV, payout, rate of return, and sensitivity reasonableness.
- Comparison against prior case or source system.

## Review Output

List findings by severity and include the evidence or assumption behind each finding.
